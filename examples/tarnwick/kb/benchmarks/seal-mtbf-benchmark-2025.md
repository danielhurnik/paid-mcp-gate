# Mechanical seal MTBF benchmark 2025

> Fictional sample content for the paid-mcp-gate demo. The numbers are invented for illustration and must not be used for real decisions.

## Method

The survey covers 6,900 mechanical seals at 412 client sites (refining, chemicals, power, mining and water), with replacement records from 2019 to 2024. MTBF is the median time between seal replacements in each group, in months. Groups with fewer than 150 seals are not reported.

## MTBF by arrangement and service

| Seal arrangement and flush plan | Service | Median MTBF (months) | Seals |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single seal, Plan 11 | Clean water | 38 | 1,210 |
| Single seal, Plan 11 | Light hydrocarbons | 22 | 640 |
| Dual unpressurised, Plan 52 | Light hydrocarbons | 44 | 380 |
| Dual pressurised, Plan 53A | Light hydrocarbons | 51 | 410 |
| Single seal, Plan 32 (external flush) | Slurry | 9 | 290 |
| Dual pressurised, Plan 53B | Slurry | 17 | 180 |

## Findings

- **Cartridge seals** lasted about 35% longer than component seals in the same service, mainly because installation errors were less frequent.
- **Top failure modes**: dry running (27%), flush plan problems such as blocked orifices or wrong flow (22%), installation errors (18%), excessive vibration (12%), chemical attack (9%), other (12%).
- **Sites that record the failure mode** for every replacement reached about twice the median MTBF of sites that do not.
- Upgrading from a single seal to a dual pressurised arrangement in light hydrocarbon service more than doubled median MTBF (22 to 51 months).

## How to use these numbers

Use the benchmark to set expectations and to spot outliers: a seal population running well below the median for its group usually has a systematic problem, most often in the flush plan or in installation practice.
