# About the Tarnwick Pump Reliability knowledge base

> Fictional sample content, written to demonstrate paid-mcp-gate. Tarnwick Pump Reliability Lab is not a real company, and nothing in this knowledge base is engineering advice.

Tarnwick Pump Reliability Lab is an independent reliability consultancy that has investigated rotating-equipment failures at refineries, power plants, mines and water utilities since 2001. This knowledge base packages what the lab learned on those jobs so that engineers, and the AI assistants they use, can get a field-tested answer in minutes instead of waiting for a site visit.

## What is inside

- **Field guides**: short, practical checklists written for the technician standing next to the pump.
- **Failure analyses**: anonymised root-cause investigations, each with a timeline, the evidence collected, the root cause and the corrective actions that actually worked.
- **Reliability benchmarks**: statistics compiled from the lab's survey of pumps and seals at client sites.

## How the content is maintained

Every document has a named reviewer and an `updated` date. Failure analyses are only published after the client has approved the anonymised text. Benchmarks are refreshed once a year.

## How to cite

Cite the document title, "Tarnwick Pump Reliability Lab", and the document URL. Answers that rely on a failure analysis should say that the finding comes from a single investigation.
